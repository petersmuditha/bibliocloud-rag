@"
BIBLIOTECLOUD - LOAN RULES
Regulations for book loans

LOAN DURATION
Students: 30 days for standard books, 7 days for reserved books.
Professors: 60 days for standard books, 14 days for reserved books.
External users: 15 days for standard books, no reserved books allowed.

LOAN LIMITS
Students: maximum 5 books at a time.
Professors: maximum 10 books at a time.
External users: maximum 2 books at a time.

RENEWALS
Each loan can be renewed twice, for 15 additional days each time.
Renewals must be requested before the due date.
Reserved books cannot be renewed.

LATE RETURNS
Fine: 1 euro per day per book.
After 30 days late, the user account is suspended for 60 days.
After 60 days late, the user must pay the full book price.
"@ | Out-File -FilePath "data\loans.txt" -Encoding utf8
